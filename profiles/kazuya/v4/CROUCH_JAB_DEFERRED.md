# Crouch Jab loops — deferred design only

The owner requires extremely precise pinky-to-index/thumb rolling motions and
is concerned that release transitions may accidentally produce Up-Smash or Z.
Physical ergonomics, button assignment and a safe transition matrix are undecided.

Do not repurpose LT3/LT4, introduce RF5/RF6 press-only loop states, replace the
four-button neutral state with down-back, force C-stick Down, suppress ordinary
actions globally, or add timers/history/frame counters/buffer simulation/macros.
No executable loop conditions, bindings, effects or tables exist in v4. A separate
owner-approved design and validation scope is required before implementation.
