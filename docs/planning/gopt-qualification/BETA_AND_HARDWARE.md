# Mk6 beta qualification and proposed physical acceptance

**Recommendation: hold public open beta.** The primary intended use is Super Smash Bros. Ultimate through the GameCube adapter. USB/XInput is a practical first diagnostic route for advertised version, executing identity and preservation. Passing that route alone cannot qualify the primary use. The minimum proposed qualification configuration is one identified Glyph Mk6, its recorded adapter/receiver and the exact reviewed UF2. Broader device/adapter, console and firmware-version combinations require their own evidence.

## Handoff boundary

Status is HARDWARE_TEST_REQUIRED for every firmware group, H3 for storage reads and H2 for input/display. Physical result is unset. No device operation, receipt, hardware PASS or release occurred in GOPT. A future owner decision must name the candidate Git SHA and preserved UF2 SHA-256 in the qualification report, the identified board and adapter, the approved operations, preservation evidence and stop conditions. The proposed sequence is preflight and backup, authorized manual install and identity check, matched startup trials, XInput diagnostics, GameCube/Ultimate gameplay, dashboard and final readback. If a mismatch, freeze or integrity failure occurs, retain the evidence and stop that session. Recovery writes or erase/restore are separate decisions. A new build or changed source needs new correspondence. Canonical promotion and public release each remain later owner decisions.
