# History

## How to read this history

This is selected chronology, not an exhaustive journal or commit log.
[CURRENT_STATE](../CURRENT_STATE.md) owns present truth; [DECISIONS](DECISIONS.md)
owns durable rationale; [HARDWARE_ACCEPTANCE](HARDWARE_ACCEPTANCE.md) owns scoped
physical evidence; [SOURCE_LINEAGE](SOURCE_LINEAGE.md) owns source/artifact
relationships. Original packets remain evidence at their existing paths, reached
through the [evidence index](../evidence/README.md). Historical pending statements
record what was known then; a later outcome does not rewrite them.

## Foundation and inherited firmware

Glyph builds on HayBox controller firmware. The [architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md)
describes the inherited/shared mode, backend and platform structure; upstream
copyright/license attribution remains in the repository. This history does not
reconstruct an unsupported pre-project story. Senscope is a separate product
owning game semantics, datasets and solver authority.

## Important failures and corrections

**Generic active publication, June 28–29.** The
[dedicated active-storage attempt](../runtime_config/diagnostic_active_storage_published_hardware_failure_2026-06-28.md)
and [immutable generated baseline-view attempt](../runtime_config/diagnostic_generated_source_owned_baseline_active_hardware_failure_2026-06-29.md)
both failed physically. The latter also prevents treating RAM backing alone as
the established explanation. Low-level root cause remains UNPROVEN. Their lasting
boundary is source-owned realization with stable generic active publication;
the separately approved native lane arrived later.

**Generated contents, July 19.** The
[canonical-grid candidate](../calibration/generated_canonical_grid_candidate_hardware_result_2026-07-19.md)
failed modifier magnitude cases. The [source diff diagnosis](../runtime_config/source_owned_candidate_generation_diff_diagnosis.md)
found 26 replaced tables, distinct from the already accepted alias mechanism.
The resulting full-replacement/overlay-preserve/reject policy prevents example
values from silently filling unspecified production tables. Content diagnosis is
not proof of every underlying physical mechanism.

**Source classification correction, June 6.** The
[misattribution correction](../calibration/glyph_external_remapper_misattribution_correction_2026-06-06.md)
established that the supplied exports came from the official Glyph configurator.
That corpus became primary; external-remapper records remain quarantined unless
independently source-backed. Historical incorrect attribution is not new authority.

**Selector failure, October 22.** The
[original GameCube freeze](../runtime_config/controller_profile_selector/hardware_failure_20261022/evidence.json)
remains FAIL. The later accepted repair does not erase it, nor prove the original
root cause. A source-identified consumer-progress gap and a physical observation
are distinct evidence.

**Source audit versus physical USB finding, November 17.** The earlier audit's
“none identified” conclusion was superseded by physical XInput smoke and the
[bounded source repair](../planning/final-usb-gamepad/SOURCE_AUDIT.md). This does
not turn the earlier audit into physical proof. The
[accepted corrections](../planning/final-usb-gamepad/acceptance/PHYSICAL_RESULT.json)
retain the initial apparent Pico-only failure, later working Windows identity,
corrected DInput/profile-index inference, and first final-readback timeout when
Configurator had not yet been entered. The subsequent capture passed. Browser
Gamepad/JoyCheck discrepancy remains unresolved, with root cause UNKNOWN.

## Current first-beta transition

The tested production source was committed before build; its retained UF2 was
then tested, followed by source-free acceptance publication and later canonical
documentation/host-tool descendants. A later commit is not the tested source,
and a later build is not the retained tested artifact. Exact identities and
mechanically verified ancestry belong in [SOURCE_LINEAGE](SOURCE_LINEAGE.md).

The accepted packet's pending-integration text describes its publication time.
[CURRENT_STATE](../CURRENT_STATE.md) records completed source integration and the
unactivated freeze/unreleased product state. Use [HARDWARE_ACCEPTANCE](HARDWARE_ACCEPTANCE.md)
for route limits and profile acceptance, rather than extending a historical PASS.
