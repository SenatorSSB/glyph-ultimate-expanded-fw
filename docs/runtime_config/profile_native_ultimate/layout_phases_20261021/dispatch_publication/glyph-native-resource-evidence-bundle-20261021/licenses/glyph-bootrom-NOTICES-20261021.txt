Private static-analysis evidence for RP2040 firmware stack accounting.
Official source/release URLs, revisions and byte hashes are in glyph-bootrom-license-provenance-20261021.json.
The linked target ROM revision was not observed; analysis covers published B0/B1/B2 separately.
Copies of original licenses and separately licensed numeric source headers follow unchanged.
